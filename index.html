About Us

After Hours is a book club that focuses on the scary side of literature, from gothic novels to Lovecraftian horror. We meet twice a month to discuss what we've covered so far, and to announce the next book on the list.

Schedule

We meet on the first and third Sundays of each month from 5:00pm - 8:00pm at the main branch of the public library.
